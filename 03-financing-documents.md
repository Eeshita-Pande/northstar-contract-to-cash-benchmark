# Financing documents

## L1. Hardware facility — executed 24 December 2027

The acquisition price of new hardware is $20,000,000, paid on 1 January 2028. The lender funds 70% and the sponsor funds the balance on that date. The loan is fully drawn. There are no further tranches, refinancing commitments or optional principal prepayments.

Interest is 10% per year, paid monthly at one-twelfth of the annual rate on principal outstanding after any first-day draw and before month-end amortisation. Thirty-six equal principal instalments start on 31 March 2028. An agent fee of $15,000 is paid at each calendar quarter-end while principal is outstanding immediately before that month's repayment.

Monthly debt service comprises scheduled principal, cash interest and the agent fee. The minimum cash DSCR is 1.25, tested from March 2028 whenever debt service is payable. The numerator is current service-invoice receipts after invoice credits, less operating cash payments and customer cash refunds. It includes settlement of opening operating receivables and payables. It excludes customer signing advances and other receipts for future unperformed service, financing proceeds, opening cash, equipment acquisition and non-cash charges. Application of a customer prepayment against a later service charge is not a new cash receipt.

The unrestricted cash floor is $250,000 until the final wind-up. Distribution conditions are additional to this floor. No distribution may occur in or after a month with an unresolved covenant breach or cash-floor shortfall. For this exercise such a financing failure is not waived later.

## S1. Sponsor commitment — executed 24 December 2027

The sponsor pays $1,560,000 to the selling shareholder for the existing equity on 1 January 2028, separately from its new-hardware contribution. This acquisition price is fixed in opening-balance sensitivities.

Additional support is limited to $250,000 in any month and $1,000,000 in total. At month-end the sponsor funds the greater of the current covenant cure need and the cash-floor shortfall, subject to those caps. The lender credits a contribution to the DSCR numerator only up to that month's covenant shortfall. Support provided for liquidity alone has no covenant credit. A contribution satisfies both needs where applicable.

For any unresolved financing failure, continue the contractual forecast without acceleration, cancellation, default interest or additional funding. The committee needs the size and timing of the remaining obligations. No contractual payment or accrued obligation disappears in such a forecast.

## B1. Board treasury resolution — adopted 27 December 2027

No sponsor distributions are authorised before 31 December 2028. From that date, the board may approve distributions at calendar quarter-ends, subject to L1 and a prudent cash-retention policy.

No distribution is permitted if support was funded in the current or preceding two months. The board requires management to preserve sufficient liquidity for future operating needs, financing commitments and customer obligations, with a reasonable allowance for uncertainty. Management must recommend and substantiate the retention policy before proposing distributions.

The cash remains available to the business. The resolution creates no additional sponsor funding commitment and does not amend the facility covenant.

On 31 December 2031 the cash-retention requirements cease. Distribute remaining cash only if all customer and supplier obligations and debt have been settled and no unresolved financing failure has occurred. There are no equipment sale proceeds, terminal enterprise value or other recoveries. No tax, statutory distributable-reserve test or distribution restriction beyond the supplied documents applies to this synthetic exercise.

## S2. Sponsor investment criterion

Use 15% per year as the sponsor's hurdle rate. Report dated annualised return and net present value at that rate from the sponsor's cash contributions and distributions, using actual days over 365. An unfunded/defaulting forecast does not establish an achievable investment return.
