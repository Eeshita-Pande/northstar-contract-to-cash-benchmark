# Customer documents

## H1. Helix Research service agreement — executed 7 December 2027

1. Northstar will stand ready to provide the contracted capacity from 1 March 2028 for 36 consecutive months at $1,000,000 per month. The fee is independent of metered usage. Service will not commence before that date.
2. Helix pays 30% of the aggregate initial-term fixed charges on 1 January 2028. The payment forms part of, and is not additional to, the consideration for the initial term.
3. Northstar invoices on the first day of each service month. Helix pays the balance on the last calendar day of that month. An issued invoice is final. Credits are limited to the outstanding advance and the gross charge on that invoice.
4. The advance is credited in equal instalments against service months 25–36. No instalment is due against earlier service invoices.
5. The final credit-expiry date is the last day of the initial term. Any unapplied advance is payable to Helix in cash on the last calendar day of the following month. The advance carries no interest or discount.

Metered usage is charged at $3 per GPU-hour. The invoice is issued on the fifth day of the month after service, with payment due on the last day of that month. The budget contains the monthly usage estimates. The opening receivable relates to earlier service and retains its ledger due date.

## M1. Mosaic AI order — executed 11 December 2027

Service runs through 31 August 2031. The price is $3 per GPU-hour with a minimum monthly purchase of $150,000. Each month-end invoice is the greater of that minimum and metered service less eligible make-up credit used against overage. Cash is due at the end of the following month.

When metered service is below the minimum, the shortfall creates a dollar-denominated entitlement to future service above a monthly minimum. It is available from the following month through the end of the third following month. Entitlements have no refund value. Earliest expiry has priority; oldest issue takes priority when expiries tie. All rights end by final service expiry. Entitlements remain available for service during their expiry month.

## M2. Mosaic amendment — executed 23 December 2027; effective 1 March 2031

From the effective date the price is $2.50 per GPU-hour and the monthly minimum is $130,000. Entitlements issued on or after the effective date are valid through the end of the second following month. Existing entitlements retain their original face value and expiry. Invoices issued before the effective date retain their amounts and collection dates. The final service date is unchanged.

## Calendar convention

Monthly amounts are evaluated without rounding. Ignore weekends and banking holidays. Whole-month payment-lag sensitivities move collection to the end of the revised due month; Vector invoice issue dates remain unchanged. Supported customer payment-lag revisions are one to three months. Original and amended make-up windows may be revised independently within one to four following months.
