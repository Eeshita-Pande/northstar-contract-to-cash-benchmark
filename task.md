# Northstar Compute: investment committee request

The committee is reviewing Northstar's investment underwriting at 1 January 2028 alongside its forecast refresh at 31 December 2030. It needs to understand the commercial team's proposed plan, the effect of subsequent commitments, and the cash the sponsor can expect to recover.

Build an editable, integrated monthly operating and financing model in Excel, including a profit and loss statement, balance sheet and cash-flow statement, covering January 2028–December 2031 from the enclosed data room. Include the original underwriting base case and the four cases in the later committee note. Explain whether the business can meet its obligations with committed resources, and recommend an implementable plan at each review date. Quantify operating economics, customer balances, financing requirements and sponsor returns, including distribution timing and value at the sponsor's hurdle rate. Reconcile the original underwriting with the later forecast and explain material differences from management's proposal and any decisions still needed.

Use the conventions of an institutional financial model: recognisable financial statements, transparent supporting workings, traceable assumptions, consistent presentation and a concise decision summary. Statements and supporting schedules must reconcile across all forecast months and cases. The committee must be able to revise ordinary business inputs and have the linked statements and conclusions recalculate without rebuilding the workbook. No template or calculation method is prescribed.

Return model.xlsx and a short recommendation.md. Cite the material source provisions supporting your interpretation and recommendation. Recommend within the signed arrangements; changes needing a new agreement should be identified separately.

All entities and records are fictional. Use nominal USD. The reporting policies and scope exclusions in the controller's note govern this exercise; external accounting or market research is unnecessary. The forecast horizon is fixed. Input revisions remain within it and do not add customers or change reporting policy.
