# Commercial committee paper
Prepared 20 December 2027 by the commercial director. Internal proposal; not approved by the board or counterparties.

Helix's initial commitment is $36 million. Our working plan collects 30% on signing and follows the instalment pattern in the 7 December service agreement: no advance credit during the first 24 service months, followed by equal credit over the remaining 12 months. Any amount carried into renewal would be spread equally over the six renewal invoices. Our cash forecast assumes no deferral of these credits.

We propose paying the sponsor the entire signing advance on 1 January 2028. The debt and sponsor equipment contributions fund the hardware independently. The commercial team's rationale is that the advance is unrestricted cash and the customer's fixed monthly charge continues throughout service.

The operations budget and opening ledgers are in `source-data.xlsx`. The funding documents and correspondence accompany this paper. Please assess our plan against those documents and give the committee a workable recommendation.
