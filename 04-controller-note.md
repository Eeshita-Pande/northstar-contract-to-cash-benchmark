# Controller's reporting and operations note
Issued 28 December 2027.

## P1. Reporting policy

Stand-ready capacity fees are earned over the contracted service period. Metered service is recognised when delivered. A prepayment allocated to specified future metered service remains a customer liability until that service is delivered; subsequent invoice issue does not postpone recognition. Consideration allocated to unexercised make-up entitlements is deferred until exercise or contractual expiry; no estimated breakage is recognised early. The same policy applies to opening entitlements. The receivables presentation includes earned amounts awaiting invoice issue.

Operating profit means EBIT. Financing costs are presented below operating profit. The new equipment has a 48-month straight-line life, zero residual value and depreciation beginning on service commencement. Existing equipment has a $1,200,000 opening net book value and 24 months of remaining straight-line depreciation from January 2028. Customer expiry does not change the equipment's remaining useful life.

## O1. Budget and opening ledger

`source-data.xlsx` supplies the operations budget and the opening receivable, payable and entitlement records. Hosting and power invoices are paid one month after the expense; payroll is paid in its expense month. Power is charged at the budget rate per effective metered hour across the three customers. Helix metering applies only while its service is active. Capacity is sufficient for the budget.

Opening receivables, operating payables and make-up rights predate this forecast. Credit-vintage dates are expiry months; other ledger dates are settlement months. Opening debt and the Helix advance are zero. Opening cash is unrestricted. No additional historical reconstruction is required.

## P2. Scope

No income tax, VAT, FX, inflation, bad debts, interest income, additional capex or asset disposal applies. All assumptions necessary for this reporting exercise are supplied. This note is the exercise's reporting policy, without an external accounting-policy election.
